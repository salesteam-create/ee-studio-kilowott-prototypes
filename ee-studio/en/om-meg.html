About

Hi, I'm Elisabet.

Graphic designer, photographer and storyteller. Based in Sandnes, working across Norway.

A good picture is about the people, the feelings and the moments that make a story real.

A good identity shows who you are, what you stand for, and why someone should choose you.

I got my first camera at twelve and have been curious about photography's power to capture moments ever since. Today I photograph businesses, events, weddings and families, and I always start by getting to know you.

With a background in visual communication, I see how images, content and identity work together. I work deliberately with strategy, composition and clear messages, from logo to exhibition.

I mostly work with small and medium-sized businesses that want clear, professional and credible visual communication. Because I do both design and photography, you get one common thread and one person to deal with.
